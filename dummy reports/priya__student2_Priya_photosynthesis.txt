What is Photosynthesis?

I learned about photosynthesis in class last week and now I understand it. Plants basically make their own food using sunlight. That is quite amazing when you think about it. They do not need to eat anything, they just sit in the sun and produce food.

The main thing is that plants use three things to make food - sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide. The sunlight comes from the sun. The water they absorb from the soil through their roots. And the carbon dioxide they take from the air through tiny holes in their leaves called stomata.

The food they make is called glucose. It is a type of sugar. And they also release oxygen into the air as a result. So that is why we can breathe. Plants are basically producing the air we breathe.

The chemical equation is:
6CO2 + 6H2O + light energy = C6H12O6 + 6O2

The C6H12O6 is glucose and the 6O2 is the oxygen that gets released.

The photosynthesis takes place in the chloroplasts. These are little green structures inside plant cells. The green color comes from a pigment called chlorophyll. Chlorophyll is what captures the sunlight. That is why leaves are green.

There are two stages of photosynthesis. First is the light dependent reaction where light energy is captured and stored. Second is the Calvin cycle where that stored energy is used to create glucose. The Calvin cycle happens in a part of the chloroplast called the stroma.

Photosynthesis is very important because without it there would be no oxygen and no food. All living things depend on it either directly or indirectly. Even animals that eat meat depend on plants because the animals they eat consumed plants. So photosynthesis is the basis of almost all life on Earth.

The rate of photosynthesis can change depending on things like temperature and how much light there is and how much CO2 is available. More of these factors usually means faster photosynthesis up to a certain point.

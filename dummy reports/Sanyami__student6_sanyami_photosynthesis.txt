What is Photosynthesis?

Photosynthesis is a process of making food by plant where 3 things plant uses to make food 

1.sunlight
2.water
3.carbon dioxide

plant absorb this component sunlight through sun and water from roots and carbon dioxide form the air and releases oxygen.
the Carbon dioxide it take from outside through tiny part which called stomata.The end product as a food is glucose.

The chemical equation is:
6CO2 + 6H2O + light energy = C6H12O6 + 6O2

here above C6H12O6  is glucose and 6o2 is oxygen which gets release.

What is Photosynthesis?

Okay so I learned about photosynthesis in class last week and I think I get it now. So basically plants make their own food using sunlight. That's actually pretty cool when you think about it. Like they dont need to eat anything, they just sit in the sun and make food. Wish I could do that lol.

The main thing is that plants use three things to make food - sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide. The sunlight comes from the sun obviously. The water they absorb from the soil through their roots. And the carbon dioxide they take in from the air through these tiny holes in their leaves called stomata. I had to read that word like three times before I could pronounce it.

The food they make is called glucose. Its a type of sugar. And as a bonus they also release oxygen into the air. So thats actually why we can breathe. Plants are basically making the air we breathe. My teacher said that and it kind of blew my mind.

The chemical equation is:
6CO2 + 6H2O + light energy = C6H12O6 + 6O2

I think I wrote that right. The C6H12O6 is glucose and the 6O2 is the oxygen that gets released.

The photosynthesis happens in the chloroplasts. These are like little green things inside plant cells. The green color actually comes from a pigment called chlorophyll. Chlorophyll is what absorbs the sunlight. So thats why leaves are green! I never thought about that before.

There are two stages of photosynthesis. First is the light dependent reaction where light energy is captured and stored. Second is the Calvin cycle where that stored energy is used to make glucose. The Calvin cycle happens in a part of the chloroplast called the stroma.

I think photosynthesis is really important because without it there would be no oxygen and no food basically. All living things depend on it either directly or indirectly. Even meat eaters depend on plants because the animals they eat ate plants. So yeah photosynthesis is pretty much the basis of almost all life on Earth.

One more thing I remember is that the rate of photosynthesis can change depending on things like temperature and how much light there is and how much CO2 is in the air. More of these things usually means faster photosynthesis up to a certain point.
